What a creative engineer is

A creative engineer is someone who builds things that carry meaning, and who takes responsibility for what those things say. They move between the workshop and the page: they can wire a system together, and they can tell you why it should exist. They treat their tools — including artificial intelligence — as instruments to be understood, not authorities to be obeyed.

Why the School exists

The instruments available to a maker have become extraordinarily powerful, and they arrived faster than the habits of mind needed to use them well. Wage Beauty School is being built to develop those habits in the open: critical thinking, media literacy, emotional resilience, storytelling, communication, systems thinking, and a working knowledge of the machines themselves.

The instrument has evolved. The responsibility to say something has not.
