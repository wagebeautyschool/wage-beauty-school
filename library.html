Wage Beauty School

Library

A working library for creative engineers — the beginning of a curriculum.

The Library is a collection of textbooks, frameworks, exercises and ideas developed to cultivate media literacy, critical analysis, emotional resilience and creative engineering. The School is interested in people who can engage with the tools of a rapidly changing world without becoming passive consumers of them. Artificial intelligence is part of the curriculum, but it is not the curriculum itself. The central question is not “How do I use AI?” — it is “How do I think, create and act when AI is one of the instruments available to me?”

The material is designed not simply to be read, but to be used. A textbook can provide language; a framework can provide structure; practice is what turns either into understanding. Each entry carries a category and a status that says how settled the thinking is. The Library is intentionally unfinished — it is being built alongside the School itself.

The more powerful the instrument becomes, the more important the person using it becomes.
