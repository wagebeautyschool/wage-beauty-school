Practice  ·  participation

Practice

The school is not primarily a course. Practice is a set of questions and exercises — each with a little context, something to do, and something to sit with.

The Library asks you to read. Practice asks you to do. Each exercise opens with a question and gives you an action; the reflection at the end is folded away until you choose to see it, so that it is arrived at rather than skimmed.

The more powerful the instrument becomes, the more important the person using it becomes.
