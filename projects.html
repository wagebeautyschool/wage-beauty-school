Projects

Work that is larger than a single document or experiment. Each entry is a record: what it is, how far along it is, and where to read more.

Projects are the artifacts the school produces. Each is kept with a sense of provenance — a number, a status, the instruments used, and the material it relates to — rather than as a portfolio piece.

The more powerful the instrument becomes, the more important the person using it becomes.
