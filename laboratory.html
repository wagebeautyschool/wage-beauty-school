Laboratory

Experimentation is part of the school itself. Each experiment records its question, its method, what happened, where it broke, and what to ask next.

Failure is treated here as legitimate knowledge, not something to be sanitised into polished portfolio language. An experiment that broke and taught something is worth more than one that worked and taught nothing. Each entry carries a state.

The more powerful the instrument becomes, the more important the person using it becomes.
