About  ·  the principles

About

Not a corporate “About Us” page. The school's statement of what it is, who it is for, and why it is being built in the open.

What Wage Beauty School is

Wage Beauty School is a living school for creative engineers. It is a school, a library, and a laboratory at the same time, and it is still being invented. It is concerned with critical thinking, media literacy, emotional resilience, creative engineering, artificial intelligence, storytelling, philosophy, communication, and systems thinking.

Artificial intelligence is one instrument within the school, not the school's entire subject. The school is interested in what a person does with an instrument, and in what they choose to say.

What a creative engineer is

A creative engineer builds things that carry meaning and takes responsibility for what those things say. They are comfortable in the workshop and on the page. They can assemble a system, and they can explain why it should exist. They treat their tools as instruments to be understood rather than authorities to be deferred to.

Creative engineering is not a job title. It is a way of working: making deliberately, questioning the defaults, and staying answerable for the result.

Why the school exists

The instruments available to a maker have become extraordinarily powerful, and they arrived faster than the habits of mind required to use them well. It is now trivial to generate a great deal of competent-looking work. It is not trivial to know whether that work is true, whether it is worth making, or whether it says anything at all.

The more powerful the instrument becomes, the more important the person using it becomes.

The school exists to take that sentence seriously and to build the practice it implies — slowly, in public, through actual work.

The instrument has evolved. The responsibility to say something has not.

Why it is being built publicly

The school is being built in the open because that is the honest way to build it. Its documents carry a status — note, working draft, experiment, prototype, established, archived — so you can see how settled the thinking is. Its Laboratory keeps the experiments that failed alongside the ones that worked.

The person building the school is not presenting themselves as a finished authority. This is a working school, library, and laboratory, assembled through practice. If something here is wrong, it is meant to be found wrong and revised.

How it is made

This site is deliberately plain: hand-written HTML, one stylesheet, a little JavaScript, and content stored as Markdown files. There is no database, no login, and no dependency on any paid service or on any particular AI model. It is designed to keep working, and to stay easy to edit, for a long time.

The First School and the Second School

The school revises itself in the open too. Its first built form — the First School — is preserved exactly as it was, and remains recoverable. What you are reading now is the Second School: the same architecture, content and principles, given a form that is more itself. The intention is not to make the school prettier, but to make it more legible as an institution that is alive and accumulating.

The more powerful the instrument becomes, the more important the person using it becomes.
