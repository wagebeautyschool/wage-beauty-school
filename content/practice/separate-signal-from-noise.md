## Question

In the streams you follow, how much is signal, how much is signal you cannot yet read, and how much is just churn?

## Context

See the Library note [Signal and Noise](entry.html?c=library&id=signal-and-noise). Briefly: noise is often signal in unfamiliar units or at the wrong scale, and the useful move is to route rather than discard.

## Exercise

Choose one information stream you attend to — a feed, a newsletter, a channel, a set of metrics. For one week, keep a log of items that caught your attention. At the end, sort every item into three piles:

1. **Signal** — you could read it and it changed something you did or thought.
2. **Unfamiliar units** — it might matter, but you would need to learn something to know.
3. **Churn** — it appeared once, at one scale, and carried nothing.

Count the piles.

## Reflection

If pile 3 is large, you have found time to reclaim. If pile 2 is large, you have found a curriculum — the units worth learning. If pile 1 is small, ask whether this stream is worth its place in your attention at all.
