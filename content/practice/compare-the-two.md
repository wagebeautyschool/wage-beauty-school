## Question

Where exactly do the assisted and unassisted versions differ — and which differences matter?

## Context

The point of doing a task both ways is not to declare a winner. It is to see the instrument's effect clearly by holding everything else constant. The differences are the data.

## Exercise

Put the two finished works side by side. Go through them and mark every place they diverge. For each divergence, classify it:

- **Better with the instrument** — genuinely stronger, and you can say why.
- **Better without** — the unassisted version has something the other lost: a rougher edge, a real opinion, a choice the instrument would have smoothed away.
- **Faster, same quality** — the instrument saved time and changed nothing else.
- **Different, not better or worse** — just a fork in the road.

Then read only the "better without" list.

## Reflection

The "better without" list is the most useful thing you will produce here. It tells you which parts of your work you should keep doing yourself, or at least keep directing very closely, because the instrument's version is a downgrade you might not have noticed in isolation.
