## Question

Who chose the default, and what does accepting it cost you?

## Context

A default is a decision someone made on behalf of everyone who would rather not decide. Often it is a reasonable decision. Sometimes it serves the tool's maker more than it serves you. Almost always, it has become invisible through repetition.

## Exercise

Choose one default you interact with today — a document template, a camera mode, a recommended setting, the first option in a menu, the opening move an assistant offers.

1. State the default precisely.
2. Guess who set it and what they were optimising for.
3. Find the alternative. Actually switch to it for the length of one task.
4. Note what got better, what got worse, and what simply felt unfamiliar.

## Reflection

Was the default right for you? If yes, you now hold it on purpose instead of by inertia. If no, you have found a small place where the tool was steering and you were not.
