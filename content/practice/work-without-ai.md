## Question

Which of your working muscles have gone quiet?

## Context

Skills you stop using do not vanish, but they get slow and stiff, and you stop noticing they are gone until you need them without the instrument present. This exercise is a check on what you still have.

## Exercise

Take a task of the same kind and size as the one in [Work With AI](entry.html?c=practice&id=work-with-ai). Do it with no assistance at all — no model, no autocomplete, no search beyond a dictionary. Just you and the blank page.

Keep a log again:

- Where did you get stuck, and for how long?
- What did you have to look up or work out from first principles?
- What felt rusty that used to feel automatic?
- What did you enjoy that the assisted version skips past?

## Reflection

Name the muscles that were sore: structuring an argument, naming things, holding a whole design in your head, sitting with not knowing. Those are the ones the instrument has been carrying for you. Decide which of them you want to keep training anyway.
