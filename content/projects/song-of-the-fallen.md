## About

Song of the Fallen is a branching story set in the world of Shorigan, written
and published as interactive fiction. It is on cogdemos.ink and readable now;
the link is in the record above.

It is kept here as a project — rather than only a link — so that the work behind
it stays visible: the drafts, the passages cut, the forks that could have gone
another way. That record lives in the development log below.

## Status

**On hiatus.** The published version is a complete, playable stretch of the
story, not a fragment — but it is not the whole of it. More words are coming.
